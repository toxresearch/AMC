About this collection and the classification tree

This library combines the 1,140 Math League Grade 4 questions and 1,025 AMC 8 questions from the previously classified collections. Questions retain their source and original year and number. The two sources are not treated as equivalent grade levels.

Questions follow the saved Math League classification tree, with one primary teaching category per question. A question may also be useful for another topic. These are inferred teaching classifications, not official question tags.

Six categories marked [+] were added for the AMC 8 collection: Pythagorean Theorem and Right Triangles; Cylinders; Data Displays; Probability of a Single Event; Probability with Multiple Events or Draws; and Mode, Range, and Combined Data Summaries.

The original question images preserve notation, diagrams, and choices. Text search may miss words or mathematical symbols that were not recognized in the source. Answers and solutions are not included.

Selections are kept in this browser when available. Use the print buttons to include the complete filtered or selected set, even when it spans several screen pages.

Original questions and diagrams remain the work of their authors and publishers. Topic labels are for teaching and practice.
